\textbf{Native Lean compilation and the main theorem's axiom check pass.}
The broader independent validation in our November Yang--Mills and QCD
reviews has not yet been repeated here.

\textbf{1. Claims.}
The submission claims a four-dimensional continuum SU(5) theory
with three generations of left-handed fermions in
$\mathbf{10}\oplus\overline{\mathbf5}$, at arbitrarily weak reference coupling.
One lattice history and selected vacuum support the stated reconstruction,
chirality and running conditions. The gauge-invariant observable theory has
reflection positivity on specified positive-time tests, locality, positive
energy, Poincar\'e covariance, a cyclic vacuum with a unique
translation-invariant direction, and stress-tensor Ward identities.
Prescribed three-current readings detect handedness. A first-generation
matter current and a gauge clover observable have a nonzero connected
vacuum correlation at spacelike-separated compact supports.

The fitted inverse coupling has leading ultraviolet coefficient $43/3$,
with gauge contribution $55/3$ and screening $4$. The precise conclusion is
convergence of \emph{window-averaged slopes} as both starting resolution
and window length increase, uniformly over volume rows and cutoffs that
contain the window.

\textbf{2. Completed checks.}
On 24 November, all 16,523 project Lean files compiled using Lean 4.32.0
on macOS with the nine pinned dependencies. Both entry modules built; all
project source hashes and build-configuration hashes still match the archive.
The solution theorem's axiom report contains only \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}, with no \texttt{sorryAx}.
The reference challenge's intentional \texttt{sorry} does not enter the solution.

An independent exact polynomial check of the representation weights gives
$T(\mathbf{10})=3/2$, $T(\overline{\mathbf5})=1/2$ and cancellation of the
cubic gauge-anomaly trace. Substitution into the
\href{https://www-hep.phys.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/~yujitach/tmp/dummies/dummiessu8.html}{standard one-loop formula}
gives $b_0=(11/3)5-(2/3)\,3(3/2+1/2)=43/3$.
This checks group factors, not the submitted RG derivation.

\textbf{3. Problems and unfinished checks.}
\begin{list}{\textbullet}{\setlength{\leftmargin}{12pt}\setlength{\itemsep}{3pt}\setlength{\parsep}{0pt}\setlength{\topsep}{2pt}}
\item \textbf{Verification is incomplete.}
The bundled log reports acceptance by Lean and con-ron. We have not replayed
Comparator's reference comparison, the second kernel, or the negative and
containment controls. The log's disabled-sandbox warning is attributed by
the README to an outer sandbox; we have not verified that explanation.
Our native run did not reproduce the Linux isolation procedure.
\item \textbf{Chirality and operator identification.}
The target ties representations, current readings and
reconstruction to common data. We have not independently validated the
lattice-to-continuum field dictionary, the chiral measure and its
gauge invariance, or exclusion of unwanted mirror degrees of freedom.
The representation and anomaly checks do not settle these questions.
\item \textbf{The interaction test.}
A nonzero mixed connected correlation excludes factorization for the
specified observables. Nonzero connected correlations also occur between
composite observables in free theories. An independent comparison with the
appropriate free gauge--fermion theory, retaining the same operator
identifications, remains to be done.
\item \textbf{The running coupling.}
The normalization explicitly uses the pure-gauge value $55/3$. Matching
the fitted parameter to a conventional physically defined coupling requires
further review. A long-window average does not establish a pointwise beta
function, a two-loop coefficient or all-orders perturbative matching.
\end{list}

\textbf{Problems observed and scope.}
No compilation or axiom failure was found. A monitor bug misread the
line-wrapped axiom list; it was corrected without changing the proof.
Mass gap, confinement, particle
spectrum and uniqueness of the continuum limit are not established by
the reviewed target.
